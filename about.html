ABOUT

Data is no longer a specialty — it's the ground everyone is now standing on. For decades it lived in the back office, something you delegated to statisticians and IT. Not anymore. It runs through science, art, policy, culture, everything. And standing on ground you don't understand is its own kind of risk.

The question was never whether to engage with data. It's who gets to decide what it means.

Data rarely arrives in a single form. A dataset can sit alongside an image, a recording, a map, a material trace, a testimony, an archive. Together, these fragments create richer ways of seeing, remembering, and understanding.

Dedomena works across these forms to make complex information legible without stripping away its context.

We develop ways of organizing, interpreting, and telling stories from what is collected — creating new connections between evidence, knowledge, and experience.
