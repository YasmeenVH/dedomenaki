ABOUT

Dedomenaki develops ways of organizing, interpreting, and telling stories from what is collected to interpret new connections between evidence, knowledge, and experience.

Data is no longer a specialty — it's the ground everyone is standing on.

For decades, it lived in the back office, delegated to statisticians and IT.

Not anymore. It runs through science, art, policy, culture, everything.

And standing on ground you don't understand is its own kind of risk.

The question was never whether to engage with data. It's who gets to decide what it means.

Data rarely arrives in a single form. A dataset can sit alongside an image, a recording, a map, a material trace, a testimony, an archive.

Together, these fragments create richer ways of seeing, remembering, and understanding.

Dedomenaki works across these forms to make complex information legible without stripping away its context.
