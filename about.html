ABOUT

Data is the ground we stand on, and standing on ground you don't understand is its own kind of risk.

Dedomenakia is an Athens-based consultancy working across agriculture and culture. Two fields rooted in place, shaped by people, and central to how communities produce, create, and live.

Dedomena means data in Greek. Data is no longer just a specialty that lives in spreadsheets. It runs through science, art, policy, and culture alike. A dataset can sit alongside an image, a recording, a map, a material trace, a testimony, an archive.

Dedomenakia develops ways of organizing, interpreting, and telling stories from these fragments, working across forms to make complex information legible without stripping away its context, and to draw new connections between evidence, knowledge, and experience.

The name Dedomenakia is the diminutive of dedomena: data, but with a bit more personality. We think good work can be rigorous without losing the human thread.

The question was never whether to engage with data. It's who gets to decide what it means.
